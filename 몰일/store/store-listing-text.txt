[App name] (max 30)
Molip: Immersion Journal

[Short description] (max 80)
One page a day: focus hours, habits, sleep, to-dos and notes. Review by week.

[Full description]
Molip is a simple bullet-style journal for people who want to see how deeply they actually worked.

A new page is made for you every day. Under each bullet you record:
• Immersion: hours of deep focus and how intense they were (%), for morning, noon and evening
• Habit Tracker: tick what you did today
• Sleep Tracker: hours slept and how often you woke
• Events, Todo and Notes: just type. Press Enter for a new bullet and tick a to-do when it is done. Tap “Todo” to see every unfinished item from every day in one list.

Enter your start date and goal, and each page shows which week and which day of your journey it is.

Tap any section name to open its weekly and monthly summary:
• Immersion: total hours, time-weighted average intensity, a day-by-period chart and a monthly calendar
• Habits: counts and a calendar
• Sleep: averages and bars
• Events, Todo and Notes: everything you wrote, by date
Each summary is shown next to the week or month before.

Sharing with a coach or a study partner? “Copy the week as text” turns the whole week (title line, summary and each day’s bullets) into plain text you can paste into an email.

Make it yours: add, rename, reorder or remove sections (checkbox, number, text, to-do, immersion), and choose which day your week starts on. The interface is available in Korean and English.

No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything you write stays on your device and is never sent anywhere. A backup file lets you move your records to a new phone or merge them with another device.
